## Preregistered campaign boundary

The R1.7 validation plan was frozen before the confirmatory primary campaign was completed.

Public-safe elements of that plan include:

- a separate feasibility pilot and confirmatory primary phase;
- three distinct workload profiles;
- a target of **36 valid paired primary measurements**;
- separation of pilot evidence from primary evidence;
- exclusion of invalid measurements under predefined environmental and integrity controls; and
- no final energy claim until the primary campaign is completed, analyzed, reviewed and accepted.

Earlier incomplete R1.x energy measurements are not imported into the R1.7 primary result.

## Feasibility pilot

The R1.7 feasibility pilot completed with **6 valid paired measurements** and was subsequently reviewed and accepted.

The pilot exists to validate feasibility and measurement procedure. It is **not confirmatory evidence**, is kept separate from the primary campaign, and is not used here to establish a public R1.7 energy-reduction percentage.

Accordingly, this record does not publish or promote a pilot-derived headline energy percentage.

## Confirmatory primary campaign

The confirmatory primary campaign has started but is incomplete as of this record date.

No partial primary energy percentages, interim aggregate, statistical conclusion or Energy Freeze is reported in this public record.

Measurements excluded by the frozen validation controls are not silently converted into valid primary evidence. Retained private evidence preserves the distinction between valid measurements and excluded attempts.
